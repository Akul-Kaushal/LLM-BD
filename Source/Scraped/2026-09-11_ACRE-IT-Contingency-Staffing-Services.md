## Description

The Assessor-County Clerk-Recorder-Elections (ACRE) Department relies on its IT unit to support business divisions with day-to-day operations of existing systems and to execute multiple projects supporting growing business needs. ACRE requires IT technical staff augmentation for specific projects arising during budgeting cycles. Vendors are asked to submit rate cards for identified positions (Table 1) for a 3-year period, confirm acceptance of the SMC standard contract template, and perform contractor background checks for selected contractors. Systems involved include the Assessor Property Assessment System (Salesforce PaaS), the Clerk-Recorder system (Microsoft .NET), ACRE GIS (Esri), and analytics via SQL, SOQL, Power BI, Tableau, and other AI/ML tools.
