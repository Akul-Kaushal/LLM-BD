## Description

Frederick County, Maryland is seeking competitive proposals from qualified firms to provide business analysis, project management, and talent management consulting services on an as-needed basis. The intent is to establish a list of pre-approved firms that can provide business analysis and project management consulting services to the County. Award does not guarantee that services will be required; the County intends to build a flexible, ready source of highly qualified firms for miscellaneous consulting engagements handled in a project-oriented manner.
