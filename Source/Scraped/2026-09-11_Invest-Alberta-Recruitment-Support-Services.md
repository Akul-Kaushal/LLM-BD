## Description

The purpose of this PQR is to identify and qualify recruitment organizations that have the expertise, capacity, and resources necessary to provide high-quality recruitment services on an as-and-when-required basis across multiple disciplines, job levels, and geographic locations. Successful recruitment organizations must be able to deliver high-quality candidates, support strategic workforce planning, provide labour market intelligence, maintain high standards of professionalism, confidentiality, and service delivery, and scale services based on organizational requirements, while aligning with Invest Alberta Corporation's (IAC) values.
