## Description

The Department of Developmental Services is soliciting staffing for Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBA) for seven Stabilization Training Assistance Reintegration (STAR) homes. Services are temporary, with the contractor acting as a strategic staffing partner providing contingent, licensed, and certified BCBA staffing to fill temporary vacancies, cover leave, and supplement civil service staffing shortfalls.
