## Description

MEMA is seeking staffing solutions for operations of State Initiated Regional Shelters, recovery centers, or support of local/regional sheltering operations to house individuals displaced by a disaster event. Requires staffing by individuals vetted and trained to provide direct public services for mass-care operations; may be a complete turnkey staffing solution or partial staffing components not already covered by the site or another state partner.
