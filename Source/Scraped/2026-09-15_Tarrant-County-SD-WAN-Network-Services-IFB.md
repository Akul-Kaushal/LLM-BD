## Description

Tarrant County is seeking responses for the design and implementation of a cost-effective, highly available Metropolitan Area Network (MAN) and Software-Defined Network Services (SD-WAN) for Tarrant County offices and data center locations. The vendor must provide a seamless end-to-end service traversing from the customer handoff through the vendor's network while minimizing conversion of protocols, with diverse SD-WAN deployment, vendor-managed and vendor-supplied service equipment, across Metro-Ethernet locations connected full-mesh.

## Relevance

The scope of work explicitly calls for the "design and implementation" of the SD-WAN/network services, matching the approved keyword "Implementation" (case-insensitive per keyword rule 4). The work is a network infrastructure/IT services engagement for county offices and data centers.
