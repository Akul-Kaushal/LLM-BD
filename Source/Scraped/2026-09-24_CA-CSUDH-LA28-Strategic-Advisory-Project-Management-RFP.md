## Description

CSUDH RFP for integrated strategic advisory and program/project management services supporting the University's planning, coordination, implementation, and institutional readiness for the LA28 Olympic and Paralympic Games. Scope covers strategic/operational readiness, program/project management, campus operations, stakeholder coordination, transportation/mobility, facilities, housing/dining, public safety coordination, financial/resource planning, and post-Games transition. Proposals are administered through the CSU public bid portal (CSUBUY/JAGGAER): https://bids.sciquest.com/apps/Router/PublicEvent?CustomerOrg=CalState.
