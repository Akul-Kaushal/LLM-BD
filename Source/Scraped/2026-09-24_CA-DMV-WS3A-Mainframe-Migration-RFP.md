## Description

Pre-solicitation notice for DMV's WS3A - DL/ID Mainframe Services, Data & Integration Modernization Solution, which will extend and enhance DMV's existing Salesforce-based cloud infrastructure (Occupational Licensing, Disabled Person Placard, Vehicle Registration, Driver License/Identification Pilot). The solution will integrate with DMV's Salesforce and MuleSoft platforms via COTS or custom-developed capabilities. UNSPSC codes cited include Business function specific software; Enterprise application integration software; Information Technology Service Delivery - Cloud-based hardware as a service; and Cloud-based software as a service - Database management.
