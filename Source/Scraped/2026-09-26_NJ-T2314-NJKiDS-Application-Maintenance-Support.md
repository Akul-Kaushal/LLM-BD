## Description

The NJSTART solicitation detail page lists bid `26DPP01287` ("T2314 - NJKiDS Application Maintenance and Support for NJ ACSES") issued by the Division of Purchase and Property. The scope covers Application Maintenance and Support for the NJKiDS system supporting NJ ACSES (child support enforcement data), across seven line items including ASP hosting, infrastructure/cloud services, software maintenance, data access, programming, and software upgrades. A 25% performance bond is required, and the solicitation is set aside for Small Business categories 1, 2, and 3. Contract period 05/13/2027 to 05/12/2031.
