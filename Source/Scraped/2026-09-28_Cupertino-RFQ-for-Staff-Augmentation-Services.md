## Description

The City of Cupertino invites qualified consultants to provide statements of qualifications and hourly rate proposals for Staff Augmentation Services for the Department of Public Works Capital Improvement Programs, Development Services and other divisions. The City is seeking to secure consultants that have the required experience, qualifications, expertise and resources to perform the services identified in this Request for Qualifications, for the specific purpose of obtaining on-call services. The City anticipates awarding initial 3-year contracts to up to three pre-qualified consultants for future services to be provided for the various types of staffing services.
