## Description

Pre-Solicitation for DMV's WS3A - DL/ID Mainframe Services, Data & Integration (Migration RFP) Modernization Solution. The effort will extend and enhance DMV's existing Salesforce-based cloud infrastructure, which already supports Occupational Licensing, Disabled Person Placard, Vehicle Registration, and the Driver License/Identification Pilot. The solution will reuse these proven enterprise components rather than replace them, incorporate COTS or custom-developed capabilities integrating with DMV's existing Salesforce and MuleSoft platforms, and support future modernization.
